# MyFitPick Responsible AI and Transparency Statement

**Draft for Canadian legal review — not yet approved for publication**

## What MyFitPick AI Does

MyFitPick AI helps classify wardrobe images, extract label details, analyze inspiration images, explain and personalize outfit recommendations, answer stylist prompts, generate fashion imagery and create approximate Virtual Try-On previews. Recommendation selection combines automated scoring and rules with AI-generated text or imagery where configured.

## What It Does Not Do

MyFitPick does not promise exact fit, appearance, colour, garment availability, professional advice or a complete visual rendering of every selected item. It is not intended to identify a user, determine race or ethnicity, diagnose health, measure legal identity or make employment, credit, housing, insurance or similarly consequential decisions.

## Inputs, Outputs and Personalization

Inputs can include wardrobe and inspiration images, model configuration and model images, wardrobe metadata, preferences, occasion, weather, prompts and past recommendation activity. Outputs include inferred tags, text, item selections, warnings and generated images. Users can correct many saved fields, choose model options, retry workflows and provide feedback.

## Accuracy and Provider Dependency

Outputs can be wrong, incomplete, repetitive or biased. Results depend on input quality, wardrobe taxonomy, closet coverage and external providers. Preview capability differs by item role; small accessories may not appear even when included in the authoritative Styled Look.

## Representation and Bias

Studio Model options support multiple body types, skin-tone bands, hair textures, lengths, colours and styles. Option availability alone does not prove equitable output quality. MyFitPick should test outcomes across combinations, investigate material disparities and provide reporting and correction mechanisms.

## Human Oversight

Most individual outputs are delivered without human pre-review. Users exercise judgment and can report problems. Some Studio Model catalogue generation supports human approval. Support and administrators can investigate operational records within authorized access.

## Safety and Moderation

Input schemas, ownership checks, file restrictions, content rules and provider moderation reduce abuse. They do not replace a documented trust-and-safety operation. MyFitPick may refuse unsafe content and should maintain reporting, removal, evidence-preservation and appeal processes proportionate to risk.
